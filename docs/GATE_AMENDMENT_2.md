# GATE AMENDMENT 2 — Definition of "real medical video"
Frozen before any gated file is accessed and before the Semantic
Scholar search runs.
Disclosure: drafted after the v4 audit showed that a simulation
dataset (DS037) would rank first under Amendment 1. The amendment
narrows eligibility in line with v4 §1; it relaxes no criterion.
Definition: "real medical video" means video recorded in vivo from
human patients during clinical procedures. Simulation, phantom, ex vivo
and animal recordings are excluded.
Scope: applies to the SS arm, the SS' arm of Amendment 1, and the RA
arm, as an additional requirement in each.
